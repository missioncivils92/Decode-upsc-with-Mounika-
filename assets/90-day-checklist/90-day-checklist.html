Three rules before you start

One source per subjectNot three. Add later; never confuse yourself early.
Consistency beats hoursFull-time: ~6 focused hrs. Working: 2–3 hrs weekdays, more on weekends.
The syllabus is your bossKeep the one-page syllabus map on your desk. Check every topic against it.
DAYS 1–30

Foundation

Understand the exam, build the reading habit, lay the NCERT base.
  • Read the official CSE notification
  • Print the syllabus; keep it on your desk
  • Look through last year's Prelims paper
  • NCERT History: Class 6–8 Social Science
  • NCERT Class 12 Themes in Indian History
  • NCERT Class 11 Fundamentals of Physical Geography
  • NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work
  • Newspaper, 45 min daily (The Hindu / Indian Express)
Day 30 check: syllabus read · 1 PYQ paper seen · 3+ NCERTs done · 30 days of newspaper
DAYS 31–60

One subject, deep

Master Polity end to end: Prelims, GS II and every State PSC.
  • Read Indian Polity (M. Laxmikanth) chapter by chapter
  • One page of notes per chapter, in your own words
  • Solve that chapter's Prelims PYQs the same day
  • Start a current-affairs notebook: one line per story + syllabus tag
  • Saturday: revise the week's notes
  • Sunday: weekly CA roundup; fill notebook gaps
  • Newspaper, 45 min daily
Day 60 check: Polity done once · notes for every chapter · chapter-wise PYQs done
DAYS 61–90

Add & write

Start a second subject and begin answer writing.
  • Choose ONE: Economy (NCERT Class 11 & 12) or Modern History
  • Revise Polity a second time
  • 1 Mains answer a day: 150 words, 7 minutes, by hand
  • Structure: intro → points → conclusion
  • Take 1 sectional Prelims test on Polity
  • Analyse every wrong answer: why was it wrong?
  • Newspaper, 45 min daily
Day 90 check: Polity revised twice · 2nd subject half done · 30 answers written · 1 test analysed

Your weekly rhythm

Mon–FriPhase study + newspaper (45 min) + from Day 61, one answer
SaturdayRevise everything from this week. No new topics.
SundayWeekly current affairs roundup · update CA notebook · plan next week
+ Group 1One evening a week on your state: history, culture, economy, schemes

Five mistakes to avoid

  1. Collecting resources instead of reading them
  2. Choosing your optional in week one; wait 3+ months
  3. Watching videos without writing anything down
  4. Comparing your Day 20 with someone's Year 2
  5. Skipping revision: read once, forget in a month
