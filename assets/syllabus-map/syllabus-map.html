Stage 1 · Prelims

Screening only
GS Paper I · 100 Qs · 200 marks · 2 hrs · decides the cut-off
  • Current events of national & international importance
  • History of India & Indian National Movement
  • Indian & World Geography: physical, social, economic
  • Indian Polity & Governance: Constitution, Panchayati Raj, public policy, rights issues
  • Economic & Social Development: sustainability, poverty, inclusion, demographics, social sector
  • Environmental ecology, biodiversity & climate change (general)
  • General Science
GS Paper II (CSAT) · 80 Qs · 200 marks · qualifying, 33%
Comprehension · communication · reasoning · decision making · mental ability · Class 10 maths & data interpretation. Wrong answer = −⅓.

Stage 2 · Mains

Counts for rank
  • Paper A Indian language & Paper B English · 300 each · qualifying
  • Essay · 250
  • GS I · 250 · Heritage & culture, history & geography of the world, society
  • GS II · 250 · Governance, Constitution, polity, social justice, international relations
  • GS III · 250 · Technology, economy, biodiversity, environment, security, disaster management
  • GS IV · 250 · Ethics, integrity & aptitude
  • Optional I & II · 250 each · one subject of your choice

Stage 3 · Interview

275 marks
Mains 1,750+Interview 275=Rank out of 2,025
