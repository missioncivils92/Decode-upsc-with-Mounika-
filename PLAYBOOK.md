# Decode UPSC with Mounika: Channel Playbook

_Strategy, formats and templates for the channel. Facts and sources live in `RESEARCH.md`._

---

## 1. Positioning

**Promise:** _"I'll decode UPSC and Group 1 into a clear, calm, step-by-step path, so you always know what to study next."_

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Exams | UPSC CSE (IAS/IPS/IFS) + State PSCs, priority APPSC & TGPSC, useful for any state |
| Audience | Beginners, 0–1 year into preparation |
| Language | English |
| Mounika's role | Mentor / educator |
| Cadence | 1–2 long videos per week + Shorts cut from them |
| Business goal | Not decided yet; build audience and trust first (see §9) |

**Why this can win:** big channels compete on volume (daily news, long lectures). Beginners are drowning in that volume. This channel competes on **clarity, sequence and trust**, and on the **UPSC + APPSC/TGPSC bridge** that national channels ignore.

Daily current affairs is **not** sustainable at 1–2 videos/week. The weekly roundup replaces it. Revisit if cadence rises.

## 4. The Decode Method (template for every explainer)

| Segment | Time (12-min video) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 0:00–0:30 | A question, PYQ or surprising fact. Say what they'll be able to do by the end. |
| 2. Why it matters | 0:30–1:30 | Where it sits in the syllabus (Prelims/Mains paper) and how often it's asked |
| 3. Concept (static) | 1:30–6:00 | The core idea, simply, with one diagram or table on screen |
| 4. Current link | 6:00–8:00 | A recent news item that uses this concept |
| 5. PYQ | 8:00–9:00 | One real past question (exam + year), solved or outlined |
| 6. State PSC angle | 9:00–10:00 | How APPSC/TGPSC ask it; AP/Telangana examples |
| 7. Practice question | 10:00–11:00 | One Prelims MCQ + one Mains question; answer in pinned comment |
| 8. Next step | 11:00–12:00 | Exactly what to read or watch next; one call to action |

**Rules:** one idea per video; on-screen text for every key term; a recap slide before the practice question.

## 5. Format templates

### 5.1 Exam guide (Pillar 1), 10–15 min
Hook (the confusion) → the answer in one line → step-by-step breakdown → common mistakes → a printable checklist (in description) → next video.

### 5.2 Syllabus explainer (Pillar 2), 10–20 min
The Decode Method above.

### 5.3 Weekly Current Affairs Decoded (Pillar 3), 15–20 min
For each of 6–8 stories (2 min each):
1. What happened (2 lines)
2. Syllabus tag: e.g., `GS II – Federalism`, `Prelims – Environment`
3. Background concept
4. Prelims fact to remember / Mains angle
5. AP/Telangana relevance, if any

End with a 5-question quiz (answers in pinned comment). Sources: PIB, PRS, The Hindu, Indian Express, official reports.

### 5.4 Shorts, 30–60 sec
- One idea only. Hook in the first 2 seconds (question on screen).
- Types: "1 PYQ in 30 sec", "Concept in 45 sec", "Myth vs fact", "Beginner mistake", "Prelims fact of the week".
- End with "Full explanation in the linked video" and link the related long video.

## 6. Packaging

**Titles** (under 70 characters, keyword first, honest):
- `UPSC Syllabus Explained for Beginners | Start Here`
- `Fundamental Rights in 15 Minutes | UPSC & Group 1`
- `UPSC vs APPSC Group 1: Which to Prepare First?`
- `This Week's Current Affairs Decoded | Week 45, 2026`

Avoid: "Crack UPSC in X days", "100% guaranteed", ALL CAPS, misleading claims.

**Thumbnails:** Mounika's face (clear expression) + max 4 words + one consistent brand colour. Same layout for each pillar so viewers recognise the series.

**Description:** 2-line summary with keywords → chapters → sources → related videos/playlists.

**Playlists:** `Start Here (Beginners)`, `Polity Decoded`, `Economy Decoded`, `History Decoded`, `Geography & Environment Decoded`, `Weekly Current Affairs`, `APPSC & TGPSC Group 1`, `Exam Updates`.

## 9. Growth and business (to decide later)

No business model is chosen yet. Build trust first, then pick one when the audience shows demand:
- **Community:** Telegram group for notes, PDFs of practice questions and weekly quizzes. Low cost; good early.
- **Mentorship / course:** structured beginner programme. Needs credibility and a few thousand engaged viewers.
- **Answer-writing review / test series:** strong demand among Mains aspirants; fits a mentor persona later.
- **YouTube Partner Programme:** needs 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days).

**Metrics to watch weekly:** click-through rate (aim 5%+), average view duration (aim 40%+ on explainers), returning viewers, subscribers per video, comments asking "what next?" (a sign the series is working).
