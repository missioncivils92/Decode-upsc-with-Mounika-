## Script

### 1. What is CSE, in one line (0:45)
[On screen: "One exam → 20+ services"]

The Civil Services Examination, CSE, is one exam run by the Union Public Service Commission, UPSC, every year. Through this one exam, the government fills more than twenty services. The famous ones: IAS, the Indian Administrative Service; IPS, the Indian Police Service; IFS, the Indian Foreign Service; and IRS, the Indian Revenue Service. There are many more Central services too.

Which service you get depends on your final rank and the preferences you fill. So you are not preparing for "IAS exam". You are preparing for CSE, and your rank decides the rest.

### 2. The three stages (1:45)
[On screen: three boxes — PRELIMS → MAINS → INTERVIEW]

CSE has three stages. You must clear each one to reach the next.

Stage one: Prelims. A screening test. Objective, multiple choice.
Stage two: Mains. The real exam. Descriptive. You write answers.
Stage three: the Personality Test, which everyone calls the interview.

Here is the single most important thing in this video:
[On screen: "Prelims marks = 0 in final rank"]
Your Prelims marks do not count in your final rank. Prelims only decides who gets to write Mains. Your rank is Mains plus interview. Keep that in mind; it changes how you prepare.

### 3. Prelims (2:45)
[On screen: table — GS I: 100 Qs, 200 marks, 2 hrs | CSAT: 80 Qs, 200 marks, 2 hrs]

Prelims is held on one day, two papers.

Paper one is General Studies Paper I. 100 questions, 200 marks, two hours. History, geography, polity, economy, environment, science, and current affairs.

Paper two is General Studies Paper II, which everyone calls CSAT, the Civil Services Aptitude Test. 80 questions, 200 marks, two hours. Comprehension, reasoning, basic maths, data interpretation.

Now, two rules you must know:

Rule one: CSAT is qualifying. You need 33 percent, that's 66 out of 200. Score that, and CSAT is done. Your cut-off is decided only by Paper one.
[On screen: "CSAT: just get 66/200"]

But please don't ignore CSAT. Every year, I meet students who scored well in Paper one and still failed because of CSAT. Qualifying does not mean easy.

Rule two: negative marking. Every wrong answer costs you one-third of that question's marks. So guessing blindly is expensive.
[On screen: "Wrong answer = –⅓"]

How many clear Prelims? In 2025, about 5.77 lakh people appeared and around 14,000 qualified for Mains. That's roughly 2.5 percent. In 2026, about 13,300 qualified.

### 4. Mains (5:15)
[On screen: Mains table — 9 papers]

If you clear Prelims, you write Mains. Nine papers over about five days. All descriptive.

I've sat in that hall twice, so let me tell you honestly: Mains is a test of writing as much as knowing. Three hours, about twenty questions, and your hand does not stop.

The nine papers:

Two qualifying language papers.
[On screen: "Paper A – Indian language – 300 – qualifying | Paper B – English – 300 – qualifying"]
Paper A is an Indian language of your choice, for many of you that will be Telugu. Paper B is English. Both are Class 10 level, and both are qualifying. But note this: if you don't clear them, your other papers are not even evaluated. Some candidates from the North-East states and some PwBD candidates are exempted from Paper A; check the notification.

Then seven papers that count, 250 marks each:
[On screen, build one by one]
- Essay
- General Studies I: heritage and culture, history, geography of the world, society
- General Studies II: polity, constitution, governance, social justice, international relations
- General Studies III: economy, technology, environment, biodiversity, security, disaster management
- General Studies IV: ethics, integrity and aptitude
- Optional Paper I and Optional Paper II: one subject you choose, two papers

Seven times 250 is 1,750. That's your written total.
[On screen: "Written = 1,750"]

A quick word on the optional. You choose one subject from a list of about 48, including literature of many Indian languages, Telugu literature among them. It's worth 500 marks. Don't choose it in your first week. I'll make a full video on choosing your optional.

### 5. The interview (8:15)
[On screen: "Personality Test – 275 marks"]

Clear Mains, and you're called to the UPSC building in Delhi for the Personality Test. 275 marks. A board talks to you for about 30 minutes, about your background, your form, current issues, and how you think. It is not a knowledge quiz. It checks whether you'd make a good officer.

### 6. The final number (9:00)
[On screen: "1,750 + 275 = 2,025"]

Mains, 1,750. Interview, 275. Total 2,025. Your rank comes from this number alone. Prelims, again, is zero here.

### 7. Who can apply (9:30)
[On screen: eligibility table]

Three things.

Degree: any graduation, from any recognised university. Final-year students can write Prelims.

Age: 21 to 32 for General category. OBC get three extra years, SC and ST get five, and PwBD candidates get further relaxation.

Attempts: General and EWS get six, OBC get nine, SC and ST get unlimited attempts until the age limit. And this catches people: if you sit in even one Prelims paper, that's an attempt used. Applying alone doesn't use an attempt; appearing does.

One new rule from CSE 2026: if you are already serving in the IAS or the Indian Foreign Service, you can't write CSE again. I'll cover all the eligibility details in a separate video.

### 8. The calendar (10:45)
[On screen: timeline — Jan notification → May Prelims → Aug Mains → interviews → final result]

Every cycle runs about a year. For CSE 2027, UPSC's calendar says:
notification on 13 January 2027,
applications until 2 February,
Prelims on 23 May 2027,
and Mains from 20 August 2027.
Interviews follow in early 2028, final result around March or April.

If you're starting today, that's about seven months to Prelims. Should a complete beginner attempt 2027 or plan for 2028? I'll give you my honest answer in a dedicated video soon.

### 9. Recap (11:45)
[On screen: recap card]
- One exam, more than 20 services.
- Three stages: Prelims, Mains, interview.
- Prelims only screens. CSAT just needs 33 percent.
- Rank = Mains 1,750 + interview 275 = 2,025.
- Six attempts for General, and appearing uses one.
